Acme Corp Employee Handbook (excerpt)

Paid Time Off Policy
All full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, credited
monthly at a rate of 1.25 days. PTO can be used for vacation, personal time, or illness.
Unused PTO up to a maximum of 5 days may be carried over into the following year; any
balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st. Employees must submit PTO requests
through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for approval by their manager.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote
arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Remote
employees are expected to be available during core hours, 10am-4pm in their local time
zone, and must attend in-person team meetings quarterly.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred, along with an
itemized receipt, through the Expensify system. Expenses under $25 do not require a
receipt. Reimbursements are processed on a bi-weekly basis and appear in the next
scheduled payroll run after approval.

Parental Leave
Acme Corp offers 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and
6 weeks for the secondary caregiver, available to all employees regardless of tenure.
Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption date and can be split
into two blocks with manager agreement.
